QuestAI

A shared text adventure. Walk out of the town square, through a tavern cellar door, into regions nobody has seen yet, and leave your name on them for the next player.

In development

What it is

  • A persistent world shared by every player: notes, echoes and world events left by others.
  • Fights with stances, loot, quests, parties, raids and seasons.
  • New regions, quests and dialogue are written with AI help, and scenes can be illustrated.
  • Play in the browser, or by text message in the US.

Platforms

Browser and SMS (US), online. Coming to itch.io first.

Privacy, AI and conduct

QuestAI sends game content to AI services to write and illustrate the world. See the privacy policy, the AI use policy, the terms of use and the code of conduct. For players 13 and older.
