AI use policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

Two of our games are about AI and one is partly made by it at play time. This page says exactly where AI is used, so you can decide for yourself. It is also our disclosure for stores such as itch.io and Steam.

In the games, while you play

GameAI at play timeWhat it receives
AI Startup TycoonNone. Names, taglines and posts you write are judged by rules running on your machine.Nothing
<g0>lineageNone. The ship's computer understands commands with rules in the game, and the browser's own speech recognition when you turn voice commands on.Nothing from us. Your browser may use its maker's speech service for voice commands.
QuestAIMiriel writes quests, dialogue and new regions; Google Gemini draws scene pictures.Your commands, character and the world's state (Miriel); scene descriptions (Gemini)

In making the games

We use AI coding assistants, including Claude, to help write code, tests and documents. Every change passes the game's tests before it ships. Art and sound in AI Startup Tycoon and <g0>lineage are drawn or synthesised by code in the games, not generated by image or music models.

Rules we hold it to

  • Fiction stays fictional. Every lab, model, company, person and publication in our games is invented. Real events are echoed under made-up names.
  • No pretending. A game never claims a live model is at work when it is not.
  • Generated content can be removed. Report anything offensive to hello@getexp.com.
  • Your words are not training data for us. We do not train models on what you write. What the AI services in QuestAI do with what they receive is set by their own terms, linked in our privacy policy.
