This website

getexp.com is a static site hosted by GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics. GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when you load a page, as any web host does; see GitHub's privacy statement.

AI Startup Tycoon

AI Startup Tycoon collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no crash reporting and no server belonging to the game.

  • Browser: your campaign, its backup and your display preferences are stored in your browser's local storage for the game's own site.
  • Desktop: campaign files, backups and preferences are stored in your operating system's application-data folder.
  • The desktop app makes no network requests. The only thing it can do outward is open a link you click in your own browser.
  • No AI model runs in the game or is called over the network.

Deleting a campaign in the game, clearing site data, or deleting the folder removes everything. We never had a copy.

<g0>lineage

Playing alone in "local" mode keeps everything in your browser. Online play uses our server, which stores:

  • Your account: a random token your browser makes and keeps in local storage (we store only a one-way hash of it), the display name you choose, and when you joined.
  • Your ships: the ships you own or crew, your seat aboard, a log of every action taken in the game with its time, saved snapshots of the game, and the legacy a finished campaign leaves for its crew.
  • Moderation: bans a ship's owner places on an account.
  • Server logs: our web server records IP addresses and requests for security and troubleshooting.

Text chat is relayed to the players who can hear it and kept in memory only while the ship is running (the last 200 lines); it is not saved. Voice chat, when enabled, travels through LiveKit, a voice service; we do not record it.

The server runs on Amazon Web Services in the United States, and its database is backed up to Amazon S3, with daily backups kept for 30 days. A ship's owner can see that ship's action log and export the ship.

QuestAI

QuestAI is a shared world, so much of what you do there is meant to be seen by other players. Our server stores:

  • Your character: the name you choose and everything about the character in the game.
  • Your actions: a log of the commands you send and what happened.
  • Things other players see: notes you leave, regions you name, your party, and echoes of where you have been.
  • SMS play: if you play by text message, your phone number, linked to your character. We use it only to run the game with you. SMS play is not yet open to the public; before it opens, replying STOP will end messages and HELP will explain how to play. Message and data rates may apply.

QuestAI writes and illustrates its world with AI services, which receive game content:

  • Miriel (a separate company that provides AI data infrastructure) receives your commands, your character's name and state, and the state of the world, so it can write quests, dialogue and new regions that fit what has happened.
  • Google Gemini receives descriptions of scenes to draw pictures of them. A description can include a character's name.

Do not put personal information in your character name, notes or commands. Never send your phone number or anyone else's through the game.

Children

The online games, <g0>lineage and QuestAI, are for people 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email privacy@getexp.com with the game, your display or character name, and for <g0>lineage a ship code. We answer within 30 days. Deleted data leaves our backups within 30 days.

We keep online game data while the game is running and you are playing. If a game shuts down, we delete its player data within 90 days.

Security

Connections to our servers are encrypted, account tokens are stored only as hashes, and our database backups are encrypted. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach that affects you, we will say so on this site and contact you where we can.

Changes

We will post changes here with a new date. If a change means a game collects something new or sends it somewhere new, the game will tell you before it happens.
