AI Startup Tycoon

Found an AI company in January 2023, ship products on borrowed models, raise money, hire, train your own models, and try to still be standing ten years later.

In development

What it is

  • A management sim in the spirit of classic tycoon games, set in the AI boom.
  • The world echoes real news from 2023 to 2026 with fictional labs, models, investors and press.
  • Inference costs against what users pay is the core tension; owning your models is the way out.
  • A full run takes a few hours. English, German and French.

Platforms

Browser, plus a desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux. Coming to itch.io first.

Privacy

The game collects nothing. Saves stay on your device and the desktop app makes no network requests. Details are in the privacy policy.

AI

No AI model runs in the game. Text you write is judged by rules that run on your machine. See the AI use policy.
