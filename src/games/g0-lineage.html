<g0>lineage

Captain a survey ship out of Haven Station into the Reach: 150 charted stars, hundreds of generated worlds, four alien peoples, and the ruins of the Ithrae, who were here first and are gone now.

In development

What it is

  • Explore, mine, trade and upgrade your ship; land and drive the surface of procedurally generated worlds.
  • Play alone or as a crew, each player at a station: helm, engineering, science, comms. Voice chat is local to where you stand aboard.
  • No scripted plot: ruins, fragments and a slow pressure you piece together, with several endings.
  • Pixel-art 3D viewscreen and a generative score.

Platforms

Browser, online. Coming to itch.io first.

Privacy and conduct

Online play needs a light account on our server. See the privacy policy, the terms of use and the code of conduct. For players 13 and older.
