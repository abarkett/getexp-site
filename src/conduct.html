Code of conduct

Last updated 17 November 2026

<g0>lineage and QuestAI are shared spaces. Crew a ship, leave notes, fight over loot, play a villain in character; just do not make the game worse for real people.

Not allowed

  • Harassment, threats, slurs, or hate aimed at anyone for who they are.
  • Sexual content, and anything sexual involving minors in any form.
  • Sharing anyone's personal information, including your own phone number or address in notes, names or chat.
  • Spam, advertising, scams or links to malware.
  • Cheating with bots or scripts, exploiting bugs to ruin others' play, or attacking our servers.
  • Impersonating other players or getexp.
  • Trying to make the AI in QuestAI produce hateful, sexual or harmful content.

What happens

In <g0>lineage a ship's owner can put a player off the ship and ban them. We can remove content, mute, suspend or delete any account in either game. Serious cases, such as threats of violence or anything involving the exploitation of a child, are reported to the authorities.

Reporting

Email hello@getexp.com with the game, the player's name, where it happened (ship code or location) and roughly when. We read every report.
